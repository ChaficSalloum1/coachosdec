# CoachOS Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

CoachOS ("the app") helps tennis and padel coaches manage their lessons, students and payments. This policy explains what data the app handles and your rights.

**Who is responsible:** [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY], [ADDRESS]. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## What we collect

**Coaches (people with an account)**
- Account details: email address and a password handled by our authentication provider.
- Profile: name, sports, hourly price, payment preferences (for example a bank transfer reference or payment link you choose to enter), booking link.
- Schedule and business data you enter: students, lessons, locations, availability, notes, payment status.
- A push notification token, if you allow notifications.

**Students (people a coach adds, or who request a booking)**
- Name and a phone number or email, entered by the coach or by the student through the coach's booking page.
- Lesson history and notes the coach writes about them.

We do **not** run advertising, we do **not** sell personal data, and the app contains no third-party analytics or tracking.

## Why we use it
- To run the app: showing your schedule, syncing your data between your devices and keeping a backup.
- To let students request lessons through a coach's booking page.
- To send notifications you have enabled, such as a new booking request.

## Device permissions
- **Calendar and Reminders (optional):** only if you turn on calendar sync, to add and remove lesson events. This data is read and written on your device and is not uploaded.
- **Notifications (optional):** to alert you about booking requests.

## Where data is stored
Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase (database and authentication), and cached on your device so the app works offline. Access is restricted so each coach can only read their own data. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by the provider.

## How long we keep it
While your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in **Settings > Delete account**. This permanently removes your profile, students, lessons, notes and login. Backups may take up to [30] days to expire.

## Your rights
You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Coaches can export data in Settings and delete the account in the app. Students should ask their coach, or contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will help. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children
The app is for coaches and is not directed at children. A coach may record a minor student's name and a parent's contact; the coach is responsible for having the parent's permission.

## Changes
If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
